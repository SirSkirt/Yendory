§ 2 Reading

Before its lessons, a model can read ordinary text and learn how words and sentences go together (pre-training). One document in ten is held out and never read; a checkpoint is kept only when it predicts those unseen documents better than every earlier one.

Get a corpus

Every source shows its licence. Records are converted into Nova's own format: conversations become U: / N: turns, problems a question with its worked answer. Large sources can be downloaded in part.

Practice generated on this device

Questions with worked answers, computed from each example itself (sums added, little worlds simulated, rules followed), so they are correct by construction. No download, no licence restrictions.

Your own text

Plain .txt, up to 400 MB. Documents are split at <|endoftext|>, or else at blank lines. Text is converted to ASCII.

    § 5 Courses
    
    
    A course is a set of lesson files with pass marks, extra practice material held in reserve, and a final exam. Its grade is 70% lessons (re-graded at the end of the course) and 30% final exam; it passes when the grade reaches the average of its lesson pass marks.
    
    
    File names decide everything: the first number orders the lessons; the words Dense, Transformer or Co-op choose Stage A, B or C; a file named Practice is extra practice for the lesson with the same number and stage (an [Extra Practice] block inside a lesson works too); a file named Exam is the final exam, with [Dense], [Transformer], [Co-op] or [Whole Brain] sections. Answers may list accepted forms and rules, separated by |: animal | creature | !plant (Dense: keywords, a ! word must not appear) or A robin is an animal. | must: robin animal | never: not (sentences). Training uses the first form.

    § 6 Planner
    
    
    A plan teaches saved courses to one model, in order and unattended. Each lesson is taught in its own stage. A failed course gets up to three remediation attempts with different strategies; if it still fails, the plan stops there and the model keeps its best checkpoint. Progress is saved after every lesson, so a stopped plan resumes where it left off.

    Thinking tree
    
    
    Nova-2 does not write out reasoning steps. For every token, its lower stage runs once and its upper stage runs several thinking rounds, exchanging signals with the dense hemisphere before each round. This tree records that computation while the model answers the question above: the token chosen at each step and the alternatives it weighed, what it would have said after the lower stage and after each round, how much the dense hemisphere changed the decision, and, at the closest calls, where the runner-up would have led. Every value is measured from the model.
